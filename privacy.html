Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

Data Collection

StringKeeper does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — guitars, string installations, maintenance records, and notes — is stored locally on your device.

iCloud Sync

If you have iCloud enabled, your data may sync across your personal devices via Apple's iCloud service. This data is stored in your private iCloud account and is not accessible to the developer.

Photos

StringKeeper may access your photo library to attach photos to guitars and maintenance records. Photos are stored on your device only and are never uploaded to any external server.
